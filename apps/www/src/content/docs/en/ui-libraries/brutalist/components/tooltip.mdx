Base Tooltip now owns controlled or uncontrolled open state, cancellable open/close delays, disabled handling, Trigger pointer/focus intent, a Content hover bridge, stable `role="tooltip"`/`aria-describedby` linkage, and Escape dismissal. Content consumes non-modal Overlay plus Transition for fixed, Trigger-anchored portal positioning and presence.

The explicit Portal part remains a logical anatomy carrier; Content owns renderer portaling. Arrow remains a visual-only slotted carrier with no shape, geometry, side, collision, or positioning behavior. Brutalist inherits the Base protocol and adds feedback style only to Content and Arrow. Hover or focus the preview Trigger to open it. Tooltip supplements an accessible name; it does not replace one.

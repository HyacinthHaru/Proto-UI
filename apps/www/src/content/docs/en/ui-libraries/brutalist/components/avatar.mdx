Brutalist Avatar is the square, hard-shadow projection of the Base Avatar loading and fallback protocol. The styled parts do not own image loading or fallback selection.

## Visual contract

- The root is a square paper surface with a `2px` ink border and offset hard shadow.
- The image fills and covers the root while Base controls loaded visibility.
- The fallback is centered, bold, and high contrast on Yandu Sky.
- No circular crop, gradient, glass, blur glow, or soft shadow.

## ChatUI use

Use Avatar for participants in conversation lists, message headers, support agents, and presence rows. Pair it with Item or Message rather than embedding conversation behavior into Avatar.

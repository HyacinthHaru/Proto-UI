# Switch

The housing stays fixed. The square thumb is the actuator.

- **Off:** paper track and ink thumb.
- **On:** electric-blue track and acid-yellow thumb; position remains the primary checked signal.
- **Pressed:** the thumb advances toward the pending direction by `1px` and compresses horizontally. Release restores its full shape.
- **Focus:** hard offset outline.
- **Disabled:** structure remains visible at reduced emphasis.

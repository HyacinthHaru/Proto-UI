# Liquid Glass standalone Toggle

Date: 2026-10-14. Candidate base `e3b15349e7a1b05d0ba6ef2a228e3fa4265fbfc4`, tree `e4f5de27264977169abe66019e230449c5ab3047`. Draft Proto design-language extension; not Apple component or native shader parity.

## Gap and design decision

Standalone Toggle was missing from source, subpath/root exports, registry, catalog and public family recipes, while Toggle Group already existed. Group Item is not a standalone alias: group value, membership and roving focus have different owners. Surface is not a wrapper for the new control and no second material slot is introduced. Existing Surface definition/implementation drift is outside this change.

The user requested real missing glass effects. The first proposed solid selected treatment was rejected during design review because it would remove glass solely due to selection. The existing material candidate API has no selected-tint field; its optical program uses a fixed regular/clear tint based on foreground, so changing a fallback to primary blue would not honestly create selected blue glass. The approved bounded result keeps regular material for both active states. Persistent selection uses an underline, border-ring at the same one-pixel width, and Base's aria-pressed. Padding, radius, fill and foreground are unchanged across selection. No new shader, tint feature or geometry workaround is claimed.

[Apple Materials HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) and [Meet Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/) inform the functional-control material direction and distinct transparency, contrast and motion preferences. They do not specify this Proto Toggle recipe or establish native equivalence. The existing shared provider remains the source/geometry/readability/preference authority.

## Ownership

The prototype invokes asToggle once. Base owns active/defaultActive, controlled rejection or later owner acceptance, activeChange, disabled/cancel, pointer/keyboard and focus. The family adds only material auto|opaque. A single v2 slot requests in-app-backdrop and retains source-owned opaque fill/foreground. Enabled auto contributes exactly one regular candidate, with press deformation selected from transient pressed independently of persistent active. Opaque and disabled contribute none. Removing material in a raw-props snapshot restores auto. Disabled retains active feedback but clears transient interaction and painted shadow.

Package/CLI source-only entries and all four Web facades consume that same prototype. Previewer registration pins the recipe to liquid-glass; public demo code supplies content and props rather than substitute behavior. The shared provider, shaders, Runtime, Core, Compiler, IR and other material-consumer owners are unchanged. The generated Finf coverage snapshot still reflects its prior audited revision; refresh its source data through the matrix owner after publication rather than hand-editing the generated checklist or marking the delivery checkbox complete.
